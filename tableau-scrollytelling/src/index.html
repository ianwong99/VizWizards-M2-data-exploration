🎞️ Inside the Arc: Basketball’s Pre-Spacing Era

In the late 1990s and early 2000s, NBA offenses operated primarily inside the arc. This visualization shows dense clusters of shots in the mid-range and paint, reflecting a style of play centered on post-ups, isolations, and high-percentage two-pointers. The three-point line existed, but it wasn’t yet a major strategic weapon.

🌐 The Rise of the Three-Point Shot

As the league progressed into the 2000s and 2010s, teams began to embrace floor spacing and the value of the three-point shot. In this visualization, you can see attempts expanding far beyond the arc, marking the early stages of the perimeter revolution. By the 2010s, the perimeter becomes densely populated, illustrating how analytics reshaped offensive priorities across the NBA.
